Questions

Before you start.

Does my model need UVs?

Yes. Your model needs to be unwrapped for everything to work properly.

Does Edge wear work in Eevee and Cycles?

It works in both, but the results differ. Eevee uses ambient occlusion for its calculations, and Cycles uses bevel.

How do I make it work in Eevee?

Turn on ambient occlusion in the render settings.
